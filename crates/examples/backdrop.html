A tesseract behind the text.

The page you are reading is ordinary HTML. Behind it, a Loam session draws the cross-section of a tesseract as it turns in the xw plane. The text sits on top with a difference blend, so it reads dark on the page and light on the shape.

Scroll. The page sends its scroll position to the engine as a message, and the engine lowers its camera in step.

Messages, both ways.

The engine posts the shape's screen rectangle whenever it changes. Open the console to watch the loam-post events arrive, and the loam-state events that mark the background as ready or failed.

Reset the turn to send a message without values. Pause the turn to send a control event.

The bottom.

Scroll to the end and the camera reaches its lowest. Scroll back up and it rises again.

The background needs WebGPU. This browser does not provide it, so the page shows without it.
